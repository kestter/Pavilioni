Photo School

Giorgi Tsaava's Photo School

Giorgi Tsaava is a professional photographer with over 20 years of experience. Throughout his career, he has worked on projects such as “Dancing with the Stars”, “The Voice”, “Georgia’s Star”, “MasterChef”, and many others.

Today, Giorgi Tsaava shares his knowledge and experience with photography enthusiasts. Through his photography school in Kutaisi, he creates new opportunities for those interested in photography and passes on his professional expertise to the next generation.

Giorgi Tsaava’s photography lecture series combines professional knowledge with practical experience. During the course, participants will learn the fundamental principles of photography and explore both artistic and studio photography.

The program includes practical studio lessons as well as outdoor photography sessions, allowing participants to develop their skills through real-world experience.
